Chocolate Chip Oatmeal Cookies

Ingredients:

  • 1 Box Yellow Cake Mix
  • 2 cups oatmeal
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/2 tsp Vanilla extract
  • 3/4
  • cup oil
  • 1/4 cup water

Directions:

Mix all the ingredients together well. Drop by a Tablespoon amount onto a Cookie sheet. Bake at 350 degrees for twelve minutes. Enjoy!

Fruit Cake

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb walnuts
  • 1/2 lb dates
  • 8 oz. maraschino cherry jar (drained)
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 4 eggs, beaten with 1 tsp vanilla

Directions:

Sift dry ingredients over fruit and mix well. Pour egg mixture over fruit and mis well. Spray two tinfoil loaf pans with cooking spray. Place mixture into the two tinfoil loaf pans. Bake at 300 degrees for 1 hour and 30 minutes. Will make 2 large loafs or 4 small loafs.

Brownies

Ingredients:

  • 4 eggs
  • 4 squares baking chocolate
  • 2 sticks of butter
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Directions:

Melt together 2 sticks of butter and 4 squares of baking chocolate. Stir in 2 cups of sugar, add 4 eggs and 1 tsp vanilla extract and beat the mixture well. Stir in 1 cup of flour. Bake brownies in a buttered and floured 13x9 pan at 325 degrees for 28 minutes. If desired, when brownies come out, melt a handful of andes mint chocolates on top and spread with a butter knife. Let cool and serve. Enjoy!

Amazing Lemon Brownies

Ingredients:

Batter:

  • 1 cup butter
  • 1.5 cups sugar
  • zest of lemon
  • 2 Tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 4 eggs
  • 1.5 cups all purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Glaze:

  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 Tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tsp lemon zest

Directions:

  1. Whisk the butter and sugar until creamy.
  2. Add eggs, lemon juice and zest and whisk.
  3. Add flour and salt, mix until smooth.
  4. Preheat oven to 350 F.
  5. Grease a 9x13" baking dish, lay out dough and distribute. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until golden brown.
  6. For the glaze, combine the powdered sugar with lemon zest and juice (add a little more juice for a thinner glaze). Cover the hot cake with glaze. Let it cool completely, cut into pieces and serve.

Notes:

  • Use quality, high-fat butter.
  • Remove eggs and butter from the refridgerator in advance, so that they are about the same temperature.
  • Line a baking dish with parchment with high edges so you can easily get the cake out of the pan
  • Enjoy!

Apple Cake

Ingredients:

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 tsps of cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 6 medium apples
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tsps baking soda

Directions:

Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Mix eggs, sugar, cinnamon and oil. Core, peel and slice apples. Add in flour and baking soda. Mix until all dry ingredients are absorbed into the wet. Bake at 350 degrees for 60 minutes. Enjoy!

Cake Cookies

Ingredients:

  • 1 box of any flavored boxed cake mix
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup "add-ins" (nuts, raisins, craisins etc.)

Directions

Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Mix all the ingredients in a large mixing bowl. The batter will be thick or appear loose depending on which cake mix you choose. Scoop cookie dough using 1/8c. or 2 tbsp and place 2 inches apart on a baking sheet. About a dozen worth per baking sheet. Bake for 10-12 minutes until lightly golden brown on the edges. Let cool for about 3-5 minutes and then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely. Serve and enjoy!

Cherry Bite Cookies

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup shortening
  • 1/4 cup margarine
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup maraschino cherries chopped
  • 2 3/4 cups unsifted flour

Directions:

In a large bowl cream shortening, margarine and sugar. Beat in the eggs and cherries. Combine flour, baking powder and salt. Then gradually add this to the creamed mixture and mix well. Wrap dough in waxed paper and chill for about 2 hours. Shape dough into balls(using 2 tsp dough). Bake at 375 degrees for 12-15 minutes. Decorate as desired with icing, sprinkles, nuts, etc.
To add icing, mix together powdered sugar, water and cherry juice to desired consistency and dip the tops after the cookies have cooled. Then let it harden on top. Makes about 6 dozen. Enjoy!

Kahlua Trifle

Ingredients:

  • 1 chocolate cake(your own or box)
  • 1/3 - 1/2 cup Kahlua
  • 1 lg. package instant chocolate pudding
  • 1 lg. container of cool whip
  • 6 Skor candy bars

Directions:

Make a chocolate cake of your choosing. After cake is cooled, poke holes in the cake and pour Kahlua over the entire cake. Place cake in the fridge for at least 1 hour but can be overnight. Prepare pudding according to package directions and refridgerate. Unwrap candy bars and place in a zip lock bag. Smash the candy bars with a rolling pin. Cut cake into small cubes or squares. In a large clear glass bowl or trifle bowl layer the following ingredients: Cake chunks, pudding, Cool whip and top with crushed candy bars. Repeat. Serves 6-8. Enjoy!

Rhubarb Crisp

Ingredients:

  • 3 cups rhubarb
  • 1 (3oz.) package strawberry Jello
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup oatmeal
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • Ice cream or whipped cream

Directions:

Butter 9" square baking dish. Cut rhubarb into pieces and put in bottom of baking dish. Sprinkle Jello over rhubarb evenly. Mix remaining ingredients together; put on Jello and press down firmly. Bake for 40 minutes at 375 degrees. Serve with ice cream or whipped cream or both! Enjoy!
