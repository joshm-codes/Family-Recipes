Baked Mac and Cheese

Ingredients:

  • 1 stick melted butter
  • 4 tbsp flour
  • 4 cups milk
  • 1 8oz. bag of Cheddar cheese
  • 1 8oz. bag of a different cheese(your choice)
  • 1 lb. of Cavatappi pasta(cooked half the time stated on the box)
  • 1 small block of Velveeta(16oz.)
  • Bread crumbs

Directions:

Combine melted butter, flour and milk in a saucepan and keep stirring continuosly until thick. Add all of the cheeses. Cook pasta for half of the time noted on the box and drain. Spray bottom of 13x9 inch baking dish with cooking spray. Pour pasta into dish and then poour thick cheese sauce over pasta. Sprinkle with bread crumbs. Cook at 350 degrees for 30 minutes or until bubbly. Remove from oven and let sit 5-10 minutes before serving. Enjoy!

Corn Souffle

Ingredients:

  • 1 can creamed corn
  • 1 can kernel corn, drained
  • 1 package of Jiffy cornbread mix
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 2 cups shredded monterey Jack cheese
  • 2 eggs

Directions:

Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Mix all ingredients(except shredded cheese) in round casserole dish. Once mixed well, sprinkle 1 cup of cheese on top. Bake for 35 minutes covered and then 35 minutes uncovered at 350 degrees or until done(knife will come out clean).

Holiday Mashed Potatoes

Ingredients:

  • 3 lbs. potatoes peeled and cooked
  • 1 8oz. block of cream cheese
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 eggs, slightly beaten
  • 1/4 cup onion chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Dash of pepper

Directions:

Beat potatoes until lumps are removed. Add cream cheese and butter in small pieces. beat well until melted and mixed. Stir in sour cream. Mix the milk, eggs and onion together. Add to the potato mixture with salt and pepper. Beat until light and fluffy. Place in a greased casserole dish. Refridgerate overnight. Bake at 350 degrees for 45 minutes or until lightly browned on top. Serves 8-12. Enjoy!

Spicy Coconut Shrimp

Ingredients:

  • 2 lb raw shrimp; peeled
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • Sea salt and black pepper
  • Ground cumin
  • Cayenne
  • Oregano

Directions:

Preheat oven to 375 degrees. Meanwhile, melt coconut oil into a cast iron skillet(or any broiler proof pan). Arrange peeled shrimp in a single layer on coconut oiled skillet. Sprinkle with salt, pepper, spices and oregano. Turn on broiler, broil shrimp till they curl, turn pink and just crisp on some thin edges(7-10 minutes depending on size of shrimp).

Spinach Gratin

Ingredients:

  • 4 tbsp of butter
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 1/4 tsp grated nutmeg
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 cups milk
  • 5 - 10oz. packages of frozen chopped spinach, defrosted and with the liquid squeezed out
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 cup Gruyere cheese

Directions:

Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and saute until translucent, about 10 minutes. Add the flour and nutmeg and cook for 2 minutes. Add the cream and milk and cook until thickened. Add the spinach and 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese and mix well. Season with salt and pepper. Transfer the spinach mixture to a baking dish and sprinkle with the remaining Parmesan cheese and the grated Guyere cheese on top. Bake 25-30 minutes at 400 degrees until hot and bubbly. Enjoy!
