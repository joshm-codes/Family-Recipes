Chicken Gnocchi Soup

Ingredients:

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 large carrot, grated
  • 1/2 large onion, diced
  • 4 stalks of celery, diced
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked and diced chicken
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 16oz package gnocchi
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp garlic and onion powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 cups heavy cream OR half and half
  • 1 cup fresh chopped spinach

Directions:

Melt together the olive oil and butter in a large stock pot over medium heat. Stir in the garlic and cook until fragrant. Add the carrots, onion, and celery, cooking until translucent(5-10 minutes). Whisk in the flour, stirring until thickened. Mix in the cooked diced chicken, chicken broth, and seasonings and bring to a boil. Once boiling, stir in the gnocchi and cook for 3 minutes. After 3 minutes, reduce heat, cover and simmer for an additional 10 minutes. Once the soup has simmered for 10 minutes you can stir in the heavy cream (or H&H) and the chopped spinach. Cook for another 3 minutes until the spinach is thouroughly heated and enjoy!

Broccoli, Cranberry and Bacon Salad

Ingredients:

  • 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp dijon mustard
  • 12 oz. fresh broccoli florets, chopped
  • 1/2 cup grated carrot
  • 1/3 cup sweetened dried cranberries
  • 1/4 cup chopped red onion
  • 1/3 cup chopped walnuts
  • 4 bacon slices, cooked and crumbled

Directions:

Whisk together oil, honey, vinegar and dijon in a large bowl. Add broccoli, carrots, cranberries and onion. Gently stir. Cover and chill for 2 hours. Sprinkle with walnuts and bacon just before serving.

Broccoli Salad

Ingredients:

  • 1 lg bunch of broccoli crowns cut into bite size pieces
  • 1 lg purple onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup of raisins
  • 1 cup of shredded cheese
  • 9 slices of cooked bacon, crumbles
  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup vinegar

Directions:

In a large bowl, mix together broccoli, onion, raisins and cheese. In a separate bowl, mix mayonnaise, sugar and vinegar. Add to broccoli mixture and stir until all is covered. Allow to chill at least one hour before serving. Add crumbled bacon just before serving and mix.

French Onion Soup

Ingredients:

  • 1 stick of butter
  • 2 large bermuda onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbsps of flour
  • 2 quarts of beef broth
  • 2 cans of beef consomme soup
  • 1 baguette, sliced
  • Grated Gruyere cheese

Directions:

Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add the sliced onions and cook until they are browned and caramelized(about 15 minutes). Add the flour and cook for approximatlely 2 minutes. Add the beef broth and consomme soup and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for approximately 45 minutes. Spoon soup into soup mugs and top with a piece of toasted baguette that has been dusted with grated Parmesan cheese. Top with grated Gruyere cheese and broil until hot and bubbly, 3 to 5 minutes.

Pretzel Salad

Ingredients - Bottom Crust:

  • 2 cups of crushed pretzels
  • 3 Tbsp sugar
  • 3/4 cup butter melted

Directions:

Mix together the crushed pretzels and sugar, then pour in the butter and mix thouroughly. Spray a 13x9" pan with Pam then press the pretzel mixture evenly on the bottom of the pan. Bake for 425 degrees for 8 minutes. Let cool.

Ingredients - Middle Layer:

  • 1 - 8oz. package of cream cheese
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 small container cool whip

Directions:

Beat together the cream cheese and sugar until combined. Add the container of Cool whip and mix until combined. Add the mixture to the cooled crust and spread evenly and level. Make sure to seal the edges completely to the pan with no open spaces. Put it in the fridge to cool before adding the Jello layer.

Ingredients - Top Layer:

  • 2 cups boiling water
  • 1 - 6oz. box of strawberry Jello
  • 1 bag Dole unsweetened, sliced frozen strawberries

Directions:

Carefully mix the Jello and boiling water together until completely dissolved. Add the frozen strawberries and let sit for 10 minutes. Stir to separate any strawberries stuck together. Then very carefully and slowly pour the Jello mixture over the cream cheese layer. Place on a level shelf and refridgerate until completely set. To serve, cut into squares.

Ramen Noodle Bok Choy

Ingredients:

  • 1 package of coleslaw or shredded bok choy
  • 2 packages of oriental ramen noodles
  • 2 oz. of sesame seeds
  • 1 cup sliced or slivered almonds
  • 2-3 Tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup white or apple cider vinegar
  • 1 seasoning packet - from the ramen noodles
  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 3 Tbsp oil

Directions:

Melt butter in skillet and brown the broken up ramen noodles. Once noodles start to brown add the almonds. Once almonds are brown, add sesame seeds for another minute until brown and then take off burner.

Sauce:

Add vinegar, sugar, soy sauce and seasoning packet to a skillet. Simmer and stir until all sugar is dissolved. Remove from heat and cool. Once the sauce is cool, mix the sauce, noodle mixture, and coleslaw in a large bowl and toss. Let refriderate for a few hours or overnight then serve. Mix before serving.

Slow Cooker Chicken, Veggie and Brown Rice Soup

Ingredients:

  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 6 large bone in chicken thighs, skin removed(about 1.5lbs.)
  • 1.5 cups each diced celery, diced carrots and diced onions
  • 1 can (28oz) no salt added diced tomatoes
  • 4 cups reduced sodion chicken broth
  • 4 cups V8 juice
  • 1/2 cup uncooked brown rice
  • 1 tsp each dried thyme, dried marjoram and dried sage
  • 1/2 tsp each sea salt and black pepper

Directions:

Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken thighs and cook until browned on both sides(but not cooked through). Transfer chicken thighs to a 6 qt or larger slow cooker. Add all remaining ingredients and mix well. Cover and cook on high setting for 5 hours or low setting for 8 hours. Carefully remove chicken thighs to a cutting board and let cook slightly. Cut meat into bite sized peices and return to crock. Mix well and serve. Add more salt and pepper to taste if needed.

Slow cooker, Creamy Tortellini, Spinach and Chicken Soup

Ingredients:

  • 1 medium yellow onion
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • 1 Tbsp dried basil
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 (14.5 oz) cans diced tomatoes
  • 1 to 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 4 cups frozen cheese tortellini
  • 3 cups packed spinach
  • 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
  • 1 cup heavy cream or 1 cup half and half

Directions:

In a microwave safe bowl add the onions, flour, basil, garlic, tomato paste and drizzle with olive oil. Microwave for 5 minutes, stirring every 90 seconds. The mixture will be pasty and look weird but will be ok at the end. Add the mixture to a slow cooker. Add broth, tomatoes, chicken, salt and pepper to the slow cooker. Stir. Cover and cook on low for 4-6 hours or on high for 3-4 hours(or until chicken is very tender). Remove the chicken from the slow cooker and add in tortellini and spinach, Parmesan cheeses and warmed cream (cream should be warmed because it may cause curdling if added cold). On a cutting board, shred or cut the chicken into bite sized pieces. Add the chicken back to the cooker and cook on high for about 10 more minutes or until the tortellini are cooked through. Enjoy!

Tasty Chicken Salad

Ingredients:

  • 3-4 cups cooked chicken breasts cut up
  • 1.5 heads of lettuce washed and cut
  • 3-4 scallions thinly sliced
  • 2 cups sliced grapes
  • 1-3 oz. can of chow mein noodles
  • 1 - 40 oz. can slivered almonds
  • 1/4 cup poppy seeds or toasted sesame seeds

Dressing:

  • 4 Tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 4 Tbsp vinegar
  • 1/2 cup vegetable or olive oil

*Combine and mix together before applying to salad

Directions:

Mix the first four ingredients. Add chow mein noodles, almonds and seeds just before adding the dressing to the salad. Add the dressing and enjoy!
