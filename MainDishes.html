Beef and Pork Chili

Ingredients:

  • 6 slices thick-cut applewood smoked bacon, cut into small pieces
  • 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 yellow bell pepper, chopped
  • 3 Tbsps chili powder
  • 1 Tbsp ground cumin
  • 1 Tbsp chipotle chili powder
  • 2 tsps dried oregano
  • 1 Tbsp spoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1 cup beer
  • 1 15oz. can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 15oz. can kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 24oz. can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 24 oz. can diced tomatoes

Directions:

In a large dutch over, cook the bacon over medium heat until lightly crisp, stirring occasionally. once the bacon is brown, add the onion, garlic, bell peppers, chili powder, cumin, chipotle chili powder, oregano, smoked paprika, and salt and pepper. Cook until vegetable are tender. Add the beef breaking it up with a wooden spoon. once the beef begins to brown, add the pork, cook until the meat is no longer pink, it takes about 5 minutes. Stir in the beer and beans. Then add the tomatoes. Turn the heat down to low and simmer for about 1.5 hours. Taste for seasoning and add salt and pepper to taste. Serve with your choice of sides or toppings(cheese, sour cream etc.). Enjoy!

Blackened Chicken Alfredo

Ingredients:

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts cut in half
  • 1 cup red pepper diced
  • 1 cup scallions chopped
  • Cajun spices - enough to coat the chicken
  • 1 pint heavy cream
  • 4-6 ozs. freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 Tbsps butter
  • 1 lb penne pasta

Directions:

Cook the pasta al dente, add a little oil to prevent sticking and set aside. Pound the chicken with a meat tenderizer, then coat both sides with the cajun spices. Heat cast iron pan over a medium heat and place a half teaspoon of butter under each chicken breast and the same on top. Cook each side for about 3 minutes or until it blackens. Set aside. In a hot pan, put 2 Tbsps of olive oil, the red peppers and scallions and saute for 2-4 minutes. Slice your blackened chicken in long strips and add to the pan. Next add heavy cream and 2 Tbsps butter and Parmesan cheese. Stir constantly over medium heat for 2-4 minutes. You want the sauce to thicken up. Season with salt and pepper to taste, toss with pasta and serve.

Chicken Broccoli Casserole

Ingredients

  • 1 can cream of mushroom soup
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 8 oz. Velveeta cubed

  • 1 bag broccoli florets

Directions:

In a bowl mix cream of mushroom soup, mild and Velveeta. In another bowl, mix rice, brocolli florets, and shredded chicken. Combine both mixes together. Grease 9x13 pan. Top with crushed Ritz. Bake at 350 degrees for 20-25 minutes.

Chinese Sweet and Sour Spareribs

Ingredients

  • 4 boned-in country pork spareribs
  • Soy Sauce
  • 2 t. cornstarch

Sauce:

  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup wine
  • 1-2 Tbsps. soy sauce
  • 1.5 tsps. fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp. cornstarch
  • 1-2 Tbsps. water

Directions: Spareribs

Place 4 boned-in country pork spareribs in boiling water for 3 minutes. Remove ribs to backing pan. Brush each rib all over in soy sauce. Bake covered with foil at 350 degrees for 1-2 hours, until tender.

Directions: Sauce

Boil the first five sauce ingredients briefly when the ribs are almost ready. In a small bowl mix cornstarch with water. Add this mixture to the sauce, stir constanly. Boil briefly until the sauce is transparent and thicker. Pour the sauce over the ribs in a bowl. Enjoy!

Chipotle Bean Burritos

Ingredients:

  • 1 Tbsp canola oil
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1/2 tsp chipotle chili powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup water
  • 15oz. can black beans, drained
  • 15oz. can kidney beans, drained
  • 3 Tbsps refridgerated fresh salt
  • 6 (10-inch) flour tortillas
  • 1 cup (4ozs.) preshredded 4-cheese mexican blend
  • 1.5 cus chopped plum tomato
  • 1.5 cups romaine lettuce
  • 6 Tbsps thinly sliced green onions
  • 6 Tbsps. sour cream

Directions:

Heat oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add garlic to pan, cook 1 minute, stirring frequently. Stir in chile powder and salt; cook 30 seconds, stirring constantly. Stir in 1/3 cup water and beans; bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer 10 minutes. Remove form heat; stir in salsa. Partially mash bean mixture with a fork. Warm tortillas. Spoon about 1/3 bean mixture into center of each tortilla and add the toppings of your choice. Enjoy!

Fish Piccata

Ingredients:

  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 (5-6oz.) white fish fillets
  • 3 Tbsp flour
  • 3/4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 1 Tbsp capers, drained
  • Fresh lemon slices
  • Chopped fresh parsley

Directions:

Preheat oven to 425. Season fish fillets with salt and pepper then dredge in flour. Add olive oil to a medium-high pan. Saute fish fillets for 2 minutes on each side or until golden brown. Transfer fillets to a platter. Deglaze the pan with chicken broth and allow to cook down a bit. Add minced garlic to the pan and stir for about 30 seconds. Add lemon juice, capers and butter to the pan. once the butter is melted, pour sauce over the fillets. Garnish with lemon slices and parsley. Enjoy!

Mangu

Ingredients:

  • 6 green plantains
  • 1/4 bar of butter
  • 1 white onion
  • 2 tsp of olive oil
  • 2 tsp of salt

Directions:

Boil the plantains for 10 minutes. After boiling the plantains, squash them and add water(from the same water where you boiled them). The water will keep the plantains soft and moist. While squashing the plantains add butter and olive oil to your liking. Once the mass of plantain is squashed really well, you may cut the onion and put on top. Now you may add eggs(boiled or fried), salami, fried cheese and/or avocado and serve. Enjoy!

Meatballs

Ingredients

  • 5 lbs 90% lean hamburger
  • 3 cups Italian bread crumbs
  • 1 cup onion flakes
  • 1 cup Italian seasoning
  • 5 eggs
  • 1/2 grated cheeses
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Tomato spaghetti sauce

Directions:

Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Mix hamburger, bread crumbs, onion flakes, Italian seasoning, eggs, grated cheese and milk. Roll in desired size and place on cookie sheet. bake in oven at 350 degrees for about 30 minutes. Place in tomato sauce and cook in oven for an additional hour.

Penne alla Vodka

Ingredients:

  • 1/4 lb pancetta sliced thin
  • 1/4 c. butter
  • 1/3 c. vodka
  • 1.5 c. light cream
  • 1.5 c. tomato sauce
  • 1 lb penne pasta

Directions:

Melt butter in a skillet. Add pancetta and cook for 2-3 minutes. Add vodka and stir. Wait for the alcohol to burn off(which means you can't smeel it anymore, usuallly 3-4 minutes). Add sauce and stir. Add cream and stir. Stir continuously for 8-10 minutes. Serve over pasta.

Philly Cheesesteak Peppers

Ingredients:

  • 4 medium green bell peppers
  • 1/3 cup yellow onion
  • 2 small garlic cloves
  • 1/4 cup low sodium beef broth
  • 1 lb deli roast beef
  • 4 Tbsp cream cheese
  • 4, 1oz. Provolone cheese

Directions:

Preheat oven to 400 degrees. Slice peppers in half lengthwise, remove ribs and seed an set aside. In a large skillet, saute the onions and garlic in broth over medium-high heat for about 5 minutes, or until onions are translucent. Mix in the roast beef and heat through, about 3-5 minutes. Remove skillet from heat and stir in cream cheese. Next fill each pepper with roast beef mixture and then top with Provolone cheese. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes until peppers are tender and cheese on top is melted and bubbly.
