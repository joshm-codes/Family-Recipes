Bite Sized Meatballs

Ingredients:

  • 2 lbs lean ground beef
  • 1/2 lb ground pork
  • 2 cups Italian Breadcrumbs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 4 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1/2 cup fresh finely chopped parsley leaves
  • 2 cloves minced garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Directions:

Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F. In a large mixing bowl, thoroughly mix the beef, pork, breadcrumbs, milk, eggs, parsley, garlic, onion and some salt and pepper. Place in the refridgerator to chill for about 30 minutes. Shape into meatballs and place on a foil-lined baking pan. Bake for 25 minutes. Enjoy on their own or with various dipping sauces! Makes about 70 bite size meatballs.

Pepperoni Dip

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 lb to 1 lb sliced deli pepperoni(about 2 cups)
  • 1-8 oz. package of cream cheese
  • 1 can cream of celery or mushroom soup(undiluted)

Directions:

Mix together with a dash of garlic or onion powder. Microwave and stir until well blended. Place out in dish or hot dip container(small crock pot) with crackers.

Spinach Artichoke Dip

Ingredients:

  • 1 (10oz.) package frozen chopped spinach, thawed and drained
  • 1 (14oz.) can plain artichoke hearts, drained and chopped into small pieces
  • 3 cloves minced garlic
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 2 (8oz.) packs cream cheese
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Directions:

Preheat oven to 375 degrees. Mix all the ingredients together and speard into a 9x13 inch baking dish. Bake covered for about 20 minutes. Uncover and bake an additional 5 minutes until the top is lightly browned.

Bean Dip

Ingredients:

  • 1 can refried beans
  • 1 stick cream cheese - softened
  • 1 can chili
  • 1 small can green chilis
  • 1 bag shredded cheese(your choice)

Directions:

Spread each ingredient in order above in a baking dish(beginnning with refried beans and ending with cheese). Bake 350 degrees for 20 minutes. Let cool 5 minutes. Serve with Nacho chips or a side of rice, or roll up into a tortilla to make a burrito.

Zucchini Bruschetta

Ingredients:

  • 3 zucchini
  • 1 1/4 teaspoon of kosher salt
  • 1 1/2 cups and 1 tablespoon of apple cider vinegar
  • 1 1/2 cups of water
  • A handful of fresh basil leaves(about ten or so)
  • Optional: a handful of mint leaves(about ten or so)
  • 1/8 cup of banana peppers sliced thin OR
  • 2 chili or spicy peppers sliced thin
  • 2 cloves garlic, peeled and smashed
  • 2-3 cups of EVOO

Directions:

Slice the zucchini into long pieces, deseed and chop. Place zucchini in a strainer over a bowl and toss with the kosher salt. In a medium bowl, bring the 1 1/2 cups of vinegar and the water to a boil. Add the salted zucchini and bring the mixture back to a boil. Reduce heat to medium, continue to simmer and cook about 4-5 minutes until the zucchini is cooked through but still has a little texture. Strain the zucchini. Place the zucchini in a large bowl, add the basil, mint, peppers, garlic and 1 tablespoon of vinegar. Toss well. Add enough oil to cover the zucchini. Wait for the mixture to cool to room temperature, then pack in jars but be sure the zucchini is covered in oil. Sprinkle some sea salt on top and serve with warm crusty bread. You can keep this in the refridgerator for about 3 weeks, but be sure to serve it at room temperature.

Zucchini Squares

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 3 cups grated zucchini
  • 1 cup Bisquick
  • 1/2 cup chopped onion
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp parsley
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • Garlic salt, salt and pepper to taste

Directions:

Mix all the ingredients in a bowl. Transfer to a 13x9 backing dish and bake for 25 minutes at 350 degrees. Let cool and cut into squares.
